# What's New in Version 1.3.0

[Wiki Home](../README.md) · [All News](README.md)

Version 1.3 is one of the biggest Water Box updates yet! It introduces a completely redesigned Online Workshop, new gases, player profiles, creator rewards, new sandbox elements, additional languages, and many quality-of-life improvements.

## Completely Redesigned Online Workshop

<img src="../assets/guides/workshop.png" alt="The Online Workshop globe-and-ship icon" width="80">

The [Online Workshop](../guides/online-workshop.md) has been rebuilt into a much larger community platform.

You can now:

- Discover simulations and individual objects created by other players.
- Download and use community creations.
- Publish your own saved simulations and objects.
- Edit the title, description, price, and visibility of your creations.
- Rate creations from one to five stars.
- Report inappropriate content.
- View creator profiles and their public creations.
- Browse your downloads, purchases, creations, and ratings.
- Download your own published creations.
- View statistics for your profile and creations.

New discovery categories make it easier to find interesting content:

- Trending
- Newest
- Most Used
- Most Downloaded
- Best Rated
- Cheapest
- Editors' Pick

The Workshop also includes activity notifications, helpful explanations, community guidelines, and a new FAQ.

### Player Profiles

Every Workshop creator now has a player profile.

Customize your profile with:

- A player name
- A description
- An avatar
- A custom name color

Other players can visit your profile, view your public creations, and see your creator statistics.

### Coins and Creator Rewards

Version 1.3 introduces **Coins**, a new currency for the Online Workshop.

- Purchase premium community creations with Coins.
- Earn Coins by watching rewarded ads.
- Purchase additional Coin packages from the Shop.
- Choose a Coin price when publishing your own creations.
- Earn Coins when another player purchases one of your creations.

Creators receive **70% of the Coin price** from every sale.

## New Gas Simulation

<img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_vapor.png" alt="Vapor" width="48" height="72"> <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_plasma.png" alt="Plasma" width="48" height="72"> <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_nitrogen.png" alt="Nitrogen" width="48" height="72">

*Vapor, Plasma, and Nitrogen illustrate the different effects available in the new gas category.*

A new [Gases](../elements/gases.md) category has been added to the Sandbox.

Experiment with 12 gases featuring different properties and reactions:

- Vapor condenses back into water.
- Propane, acetylene, hydrogen, and explosive gas can ignite or explode.
- Firework gas creates colorful effects.
- Helium is extremely light and can lift objects.
- Plasma produces intense heat.
- Nitrogen can freeze materials.
- Neon, argon, and xenon produce glowing particle effects.

You can also use the new configurable [Gas Spawner](../elements/gases.md#gas-spawner) to continuously generate your selected gas at a custom interval.

## More Sandbox Elements

Several cooling and material-related elements are available again:

- [Cooler](../elements/functional-elements.md#cooler): Continuously cools everything nearby.
- [Ice Bomb](../elements/explosives.md#ice-bomb): Its explosion instantly freezes nearby elements.
- [Freeze (Cooling Tool)](../elements/powers.md#freeze): Manually cools an area, extinguishes fires, turns lava into stone, and can harm ragdolls.
- [Chemical Materials](../elements/chemical-materials.md):
  - Ice: Melts when exposed to heat or fire.
  - Coal: Starts burning when ignited.
  - Lead: Melts when heated and solidifies again as it cools.
  - Magnesium: Burns with an extremely bright flame.
  - Thermite: Burns at an exceptionally high temperature and spreads fire around it.

See [Temperature](../systems/temperature.md) for how heat, cold, and material reactions work together.
