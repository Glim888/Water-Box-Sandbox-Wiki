# Online Workshop

[Wiki Home](../README.md) · [Elements and Gameplay Systems](../README.md#explore-the-wiki)

The Online Workshop is the in-game place to discover and share creations. Open **Online Workshop** from the game menu and connect to the internet to browse or publish content.

<img src="../assets/guides/workshop.png" alt="The globe-and-ship icon for the Online Workshop" width="96">

*Workshop content includes reusable Objects and complete Simulations.*

## Find a Creation

Choose **All**, **Objects**, or **Simulations**. Browse lists such as **Most Used**, **Most Downloaded**, **Best Rated**, **Newest**, **Cheapest**, **Editors' Pick**, and **Trending**.

Open a listing and read its description, creation type, preview, coin price, and element or Creative-Pack requirements. Look for instructions on starting a machine or loading a particular experiment.

## Download and Use

1. Open the creation's page and check its requirements.
2. Complete the purchase action if a price is required and you want the creation.
3. Use the available download action and wait for success.
4. Find it under **My Downloaded Creations**, or use the available action on its page.
5. Place an Object in your sandbox, or save your current work before opening a Simulation.

See [Saving and Loading](saving-and-loading.md#object-or-simulation) for the difference between the two types.

## Publish a Creation

1. [Save your Object or Simulation](saving-and-loading.md), reload it, and test it.
2. Choose **Submit to Workshop** for the saved creation.
3. Enter a title and a description explaining what it does, how to start it, and any requirements.
4. Choose the coin price offered by the game.
5. Read the linked Terms of Use and Guidelines and accept them if you agree.
6. Select **Publish**, wait for the result, and check **My Creations**.

Include important [Liquid Settings](../systems/liquid-physics.md#liquid-settings) when sharing a physics experiment. The [Building Tutorials](building-tutorials.md) are useful starting points for a first creation.

## Manage, Rate and Report

Under **My Creations**, editing controls can change listing details such as title, description, price, and public visibility. Editing a local build is separate from changing those listing details.

Use **My Purchases** and **My Ratings** to revisit activity. Creation pages offer rating and reporting actions where available; give a clear reason when reporting a problem.

For access, version, or submission errors, see [FAQ and Troubleshooting](faq-and-troubleshooting.md#workshop-and-saves).
