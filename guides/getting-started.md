# Getting Started

[Wiki Home](../README.md) · [Elements and Gameplay Systems](../README.md#explore-the-wiki)

Start with a small scene so you can learn the tools and see what changes when the simulation runs.

## Your First Experiment

<img src="../assets/systems/liquid-physics/liquid-shapes.png" alt="Liquid shapes and a basin from the in-game help" width="320">

*Liquid particles collect in a basin once the simulation runs.*

1. Open a sandbox with a basin, or pause and draw one using [Terrain](../elements/terrain.md).
2. Select [Water](../elements/powers.md#water) and add a modest amount inside the basin. Resume if the selected tool requires a running simulation.
3. Pause and place a small [Wood](../elements/building-materials.md#wood) piece above the water.
4. Resume and observe floating, splashes, and motion.
5. Add another piece or some cargo, changing one thing at a time.
6. Save the complete scene through **Save/Load Simulations** so you can repeat the experiment.

If the piece sinks, check [Liquid Particle Weight](../systems/liquid-physics.md#liquid-settings) and try a lighter or wider construction.

## Learn the Main Actions

Pause to arrange a scene, resume to test it, and open an element's settings to change its behavior. The game explains when an action requires a different pause state.

Use [Controls and Tools](controls-and-tools.md) for camera movement, selection, rotation, and Undo. For your next project, follow the [boat, car, and machine tutorials](building-tutorials.md).

## Keep and Share Your Work

[Save an Object](saving-and-loading.md#save-an-object) to reuse a construction, or [save a Simulation](saving-and-loading.md#save-a-simulation) to preserve the surrounding scene. Publishing through the [Online Workshop](online-workshop.md) is a separate step.

For explanations of what you observe, start with [Liquid Physics](../systems/liquid-physics.md) and [Physics and Forces](../systems/physics-and-forces.md).
