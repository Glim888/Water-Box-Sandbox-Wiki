# Chemical Materials

[Wiki Home](../README.md) · [All Element Categories](../README.md#explore-the-wiki)

Chemical Materials are solid particle-based substances with distinct temperature and combustion behavior. They can be drawn directly into the sandbox and combined with [fire](powers.md#fire), liquids, and cooling effects.

## Materials

| Icon | Element | Description |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| <img src="../assets/elements/chemical-materials/gpower_liquid_geo_ice.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="ice"></a> **Ice** | A frozen material that melts when exposed to heat such as [fire](powers.md#fire). |
| <img src="../assets/elements/chemical-materials/gpower_liquid_geo_coal.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="coal"></a> **Coal** | A combustible solid that starts burning when ignited. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/chemical-materials/gpower_liquid_geo_lead.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="lead"></a> **Lead** | Melts when heated and solidifies again as it cools. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/chemical-materials/gpower_liquid_geo_magnesium.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="magnesium"></a> **Magnesium** | Burns with a very bright flame after it is ignited. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/chemical-materials/gpower_liquid_geo_thermite.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="thermite"></a> **Thermite** | Burns at an extremely high temperature and can spread [fire](powers.md#fire) to nearby materials. |

## Tips

- Pause the simulation when arranging a controlled material test.
- Place heat and cooling sources at different distances to compare reactions.
- Use a non-flammable container when testing Magnesium or Thermite.
