# Gases

[Wiki Home](../README.md) · [All Element Categories](../README.md#explore-the-wiki)

Gases move through open spaces and react differently to heat and ignition. The **Gas Spawner** can continuously create any available gas for experiments or machines.

## Gas Elements

| Icon | Element | Description |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_vapor.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="vapor"></a> **Vapor** | [Water](powers.md#water) vapor that cools down and condenses back into liquid [water](powers.md#water). |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_expgas.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="explosive-gas"></a> **Explosive Gas** | A light flammable gas that produces a small blast when ignited. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_propane.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="propane"></a> **Propane** | A highly flammable fuel gas that creates a medium explosion. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_acetylene.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="acetylene"></a> **Acetylene** | A very unstable and highly flammable gas that produces a large explosion. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_fireworks.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="firework-gas"></a> **Firework Gas** | A group of special gases that create colorful effects when they explode. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_helium.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="helium"></a> **Helium** | An extremely light, inert, and non-flammable gas that can lift elements. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_plasma.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="plasma"></a> **Plasma** | A superheated ionized gas that is extremely hot but not flammable or explosive. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_nitrogen.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="nitrogen"></a> **Nitrogen** | A cold, inert, and non-flammable gas that can [freeze](powers.md#freeze) materials. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_hydrogen.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="hydrogen"></a> **Hydrogen** | A light, flammable gas for ignition experiments. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_neon.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="neon"></a> **Neon** | A non-flammable gas with a glowing particle effect. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_argon.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="argon"></a> **Argon** | A non-flammable gas with a distinct glowing particle effect. |
| <img src="../assets/elements/gases/gases_xenon.png" alt="" width="48" height="72"> | <a id="xenon"></a> **Xenon** | A non-flammable gas with its own glowing particle effect. |

## Tips

- Use enclosed spaces to observe how gases spread and collect.
- Keep ignition sources away from flammable gases until the setup is complete.
- Combine Vapor with heating and cooling to create a water-cycle experiment.
